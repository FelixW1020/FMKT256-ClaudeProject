Data and method

Prices, macro series and company figures come from public sources; the driver profiles add our judgments, each shown with its basis. A scheduled job refreshes the data each weekday, and you can bring your own prices to add a company.

How the two lenses work

Add a company from a price file

Download monthly or daily prices from Yahoo Finance as a CSV, then drop the file here. Torque lines it up with the factor history, estimates its sensitivities in your browser and pulls them toward peer averages.

  1. Line up the months. The last close in each month becomes a monthly dollar return, matched to the factors the built-in companies use. The yen applies only to Japanese makers, so it is left out.
  2. Estimate in your browser. A regression with Newey-West errors gives each factor sensitivity and its uncertainty. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. Pull toward peers. Each estimate moves toward the peer average in proportion to how noisy it is, so a short history does not produce wild betas.
  4. Add the business. Pick a type and enter market cap, revenue, margin and growth. The fundamentals lens calibrates to that price the same way it does for the others.
